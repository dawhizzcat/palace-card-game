Rules

Setup: Each player gets 3 face-down palace cards (secret), then 6 hand cards — choose 3 to place face-up on your palace. The remaining 3 stay in hand.

Starting: Whoever holds the 3♠ plays it first. If no one has it, the lowest 3 goes first.

Goal: Be first to empty your hand, then face-up, then face-down cards.

On your turn: Play a card equal to or higher than the top of the pile, then refill your hand to 3 from the draw pile.

Special Cards:
  • 2 Wildcard — plays on anything. Play another card immediately on top.
  • 7 Reverse ceiling — next player must play 7 or lower.
  • 10 Nuke — must follow normal rank rules, then clears the pile. Play again.
  • A Highest card (below 2).
  • 4-of Four of a kind clears the pile. Same player goes again.
Multi-card plays: Multiple cards of the same rank from hand. Only one at a time from face-up palace.

Can't play? Pick up the entire pile. Play passes to the next player.

Face-down cards: Flip blind — if it can't be played, pick it and the pile up.

Disconnection: If a player leaves, their turn is skipped automatically.
