Rules

Setup: Each player gets 3 face-down, 3 face-up (chosen), and 3 hand cards.

Goal: Be the first to empty your hand, face-up, then face-down cards.

Play: Play equal or higher cards on the pile. Refill hand to 3 from the draw pile.

Special Cards:
2 ♣ — Wildcard, play on anything. Next player must play on top of it.
7 ♣ — Next player must play a 7 or lower.
10 ♣ — Clears the pile. Play again.
A ♣ — Highest card.
Four of a kind — Pile clears, last player goes again.

Can't play? Pick up the pile. Play then passes.

Face-down cards: Flip blind — if it can't be played, pick it and the pile up.
